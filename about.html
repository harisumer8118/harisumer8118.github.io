The developer behind the code

I'm Haris Umer, a full-stack developer building the systems that businesses actually run on, not just marketing sites, but the tools behind the scenes: POS, CRM, SaaS and e-commerce platforms.

My story

I don't just write code, I build relationships. Every project starts with understanding how a business actually operates, then shaping software around that.

My focus is on production-ready systems: platforms that hold up under real usage, not just demos. That means clean database design, thoughtful UI, and deployment pipelines that keep things running without surprises. Whether it's a POS system for a busy restaurant floor or a CRM tracking hundreds of leads, the goal is the same, software your team can rely on every single day.
